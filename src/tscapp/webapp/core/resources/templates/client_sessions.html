Sessions and tasks

View your sessions and tasks

Sessions hold files relevant to themselves, which you can upload and run clustering on after clicking the "manage" button of a session.
The action of executing a clustering on certain files within a session is a task. Every task ran in all sessions is visible here. If you are interested in tasks ran for a certain session, click on the "manage" button of that session.

Once you delete a session, all files and tasks relevant to that session will be unrecoverably deleted.
